THE THESIS

AI must cite history.
No company should own it.

OpenCite is a public provenance archive for books: a shared record of which edition existed, what its exact fingerprint was, who witnessed it, and when they committed that evidence.

One hash cannot prove a book's claims are true. Many independent witnesses can preserve a durable historical record and expose later alteration.

Libraries, publishers, researchers, and readers contribute metadata and hashes. Copyrighted bytes stay with lawful custodians. Public-domain works may be mirrored separately by archives built for preservation.
