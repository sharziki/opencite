# Code of conduct

OpenCite contributors must keep participation respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, deliberate misinformation, and publishing private or copyrighted material without authorization are not accepted. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect contributors and the project.

Report conduct concerns privately through repository security reporting. Maintainers will review reports promptly, protect reporter privacy where possible, and document moderation decisions that can safely be public.
